Review scope

This is an interactive art-direction sample, using STARFALL's actual native WebGL meshes and renderer. The industrial deck and effects are original geometry. Encounters and bullet paths are scripted visual studies: there are no collisions, rewards, damage changes or saved progression here.

The live game's single index.html, assets, campaign, balance and boss kits are untouched. The footage informs pacing, readability and presentation; its artwork, UI, characters and music are not reused.
