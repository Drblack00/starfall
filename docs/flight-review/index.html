01 / SQUAD TACTICS

Arrivals with weight and readable intent

Warning first, engines flare, ships bank into position, then engage. No arrival fire hidden behind a flash. The prototypes illustrate regrouping existing enemies, not added spawn counts.

Recommended rotation: Spearhead for first contact, Crossfire for pressure, Escort for priority targeting. Keep a visible escape lane and cap simultaneous arrival effects.
02 / VICTORY INTO HYPERSPACE

One continuous cinematic handoff

Boss reactors fail in stages. Debris settles, the ship aligns, space stretches, then the next sector resolves. UI retracts during travel and returns after arrival.

04 / FIFTEEN HULLS, FIFTEEN IDENTITIES

Different decisions, not just different bullet colors.

Proposed mechanics, not live changes. Damage, duration, cooldown, boss interactions and competitive limits need a separate balance pass. Existing baseline stats remain frozen.

05 / ORIGINAL ACTION SCORE STUDIES

Choose the sound of the next flight

Three original synthesized sketches. These short loops test rhythm and mood; final production would add richer instruments and combat-aware layering. Nothing autoplays.

My recommendation: Orbital Assault for waves, Reactor Siege for boss layers, and a filtered engine rise into a clean musical release on the hyperspace jump.
